How the draw works

1. One application per person

Applications are matched on the national ID number, and also on close name and date of birth matches in case a digit was mistyped. A suspected repeat is held for an officer to review rather than thrown out by the machine.

2. A lottery you can recompute

Your position is the SHA-256 hash of the published seed and your application number. No random number generator is involved, so the same list comes out every time, on any machine.

3. Reasons, not just outcomes

Once the result is published, your status page shows your position, the cutoff in your category, and the step-by-step record of how the decision was reached.
